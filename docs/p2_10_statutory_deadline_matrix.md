## 2. In-Depth Statutory Analysis by Domain

### 2.1 EPF / ECR (Employees' Provident Fund)
- **Legal Authority**: Employees' Provident Fund Organisation (EPFO), Ministry of Labour and Employment, Government of India.
- **Governing Provision**: Paragraph 38(1) of the Employees' Provident Funds Scheme, 1952.
- **Due Date Rule**: "The employer shall pay to the Fund both the contribution payable by himself... and also, on behalf of the member employed by him, within fifteen days of the close of every month".
- **Grace Period Abolition**: The former 5-day grace period was officially abolished by EPFO Circular No. WDU/7(1)2006/ECR/Part dated 08.01.2016, effective February 2016. Due date is strictly the 15th.
- **Applicability**: All establishments registered under EPF Act employing 20 or more employees.

### 2.2 ESIC (Employees' State Insurance Corporation)
- **Legal Authority**: Employees' State Insurance Corporation, Ministry of Labour and Employment, Government of India.
- **Governing Provision**: Regulation 31 of the Employees' State Insurance (General) Regulations, 1950.
- **Due Date Rule**: Following the amendment vide Gazette Notification No. N-12/13/1/2016-P&D dated 03.05.2017, contribution payment was changed from the 21st to within **15 days** of the last day of the calendar month in which contributions fall due.
- **Applicability**: Covered establishments employing eligible employees earning monthly gross salary up to ₹21,000 (₹25,000 for employees with disability).

### 2.3 TDS (Tax Deducted at Source — Form 24Q & Monthly Deposits)
- **Legal Authority**: Central Board of Direct Taxes (CBDT), Department of Revenue, Ministry of Finance.
- **Monthly Deposit**:
  - Income-tax Rules, 1962, Rule 30(2)(a):
    - Sub-clause (i): Sum deducted from April through February shall be paid to the credit of the Central Government on or before the **7th day of the month immediately following**.
    - Sub-clause (ii): Sum deducted in the month of March shall be paid on or before the **30th day of April**.
- **Quarterly Statements (Form 24Q)**:
  - Income-tax Rules, 1962, Rule 31A(2):
    - Quarter 1 (ending 30th June): **31st July**
    - Quarter 2 (ending 30th September): **31st October**
    - Quarter 3 (ending 31st December): **31st January**
    - Quarter 4 (ending 31st March): **31st May**

### 2.4 Multi-State Professional Tax (KA, MH, TS, WB)
- **Karnataka**:
  - Section 6A(1) of the Karnataka PT Act, 1976: "Every employer registered under this Act shall submit to the assessing authority a return in the prescribed form on or before the **twentieth of every month**".
- **Maharashtra**:
  - Rule 11(1) of the Maharashtra State Tax on Professions, Trades, Callings and Employments Rules, 1975: "Every employer... shall submit monthly return in Form III-B on or before the **last day of the month** following the month for which the return relates".
  - OrbitHR correctly computes variable month length (30 days for April/June/Sept/Nov; 31 days for Jan/Mar/May/July/Aug/Oct/Dec; 28 or 29 days for Feb).
- **Telangana**:
  - Section 7(1) of the Telangana PT Act, 1987 read with Rule 12: Monthly return in Form V and payment on or before the **10th day of the following month**.
- **West Bengal**:
  - Section 6 of the West Bengal PT Act, 1979 read with Rule 12: Monthly return and tax deposit via GRIPS on or before the **21st day of the succeeding month**.

## 3. Statutory Deadline vs. Internal Target Date

| Concept | Definition | Can Tenant Admin Edit? | Masquerades as Legal Due Date? | Provenance / Audit Logged? |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Statutory Due Date** | Unalterable legal deadline codified by Act/Rule | **NO** | N/A (Is the real legal date) | Hardcoded deterministic rule |
| **Officially Extended Due Date** | Legally revised deadline issued by Government Circular | **NO (System / Super Admin only)** | Replaces effective calculation | Preserves official circular title & date |
| **Internal Target Date** | Operational goal set by HR/Finance to complete work ahead of time | **YES** | **NEVER** | Yes (`AuditLog` event) |

## 4. Unsupported & Deferred Obligations

The following obligations are explicitly out of scope for P2.10 and marked deferred:
1. **Tamil Nadu & Gujarat Professional Tax**: Awaiting separate multi-state adapter approval (P2.9 freeze scope retained).
2. **Labour Welfare Fund (LWF)**: Semi-annual / annual filing schedules deferred to dedicated LWF module.
3. **Goods and Services Tax (GST)**: Out of HRMS scope.
4. **Gratuity & Bonus Annual Returns (Form D)**: Deferred.
